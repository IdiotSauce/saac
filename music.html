My Music!

Dr. Skumbulge's Pretty Huge Discography

Dr. Skumbulge's PHD is a compilation album of 15 random songs I made between the years of 2019 to early 2025. Most of the songs were written on my childhood iPad, using GarageBand. A lot of the songs are based on, or were made through the inspiration of, various dumb inside jokes between me and my girlfriend. Three of these songs feature titular character Dr. Skumbulge, who is a strange creature based on an old deviantart FNAF photoshop image. Everything he says is contradictory, and he has a lot to say about eating people. you can read more about him here.

Dr. Skumbulge's PHD is available on most music streaming services, including Spotify, Apple Music, and Youtube. Additionally, you can also buy this album and support me on Bandcamp!!! The song featured in my game BrainShrill, "Cold-Headed", is also available there as well!!!
